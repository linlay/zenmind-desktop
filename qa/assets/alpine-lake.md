# 图片背景演示素材

`alpine-lake.png` 是通过内置 imagegen 工具生成的山湖照片风格壁纸，供独立预览和真实图片导入验收使用，不作为用户默认背景打包到产品中。预览地址附加 `?background=photo` 会直接展示这张图片；设置页仍可导入其他 PNG/JPEG。

生成提示词：

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create one realistic landscape photograph to use as a desktop app background wallpaper. Wide 16:10 composition, a crystal clear turquoise alpine lake with ripples across the lower half, unmistakable rugged mountain peaks and evergreen forest along the far shoreline across the middle, a little soft morning sky at the top. Natural detailed photography, vivid but tasteful teal blue water and deep forest green, soft sunlight on the peaks. The scene must clearly read as a real photographic picture rather than an abstract gradient, vector illustration, or painted texture. No text, no logos, no user interface, no frames. Deliver just the wallpaper image, approximately 1920 by 1200 pixels.
